Community Rules

Help keep Yappo safe and respectful.

• Treat other users with respect.

• Do not harass, threaten, or bully anyone.

• Never share passwords, verification codes, or financial details.

• Do not record or share another person's video without their consent.

• End the chat if you feel uncomfortable.

• Report abuse when reporting features become available.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start chatting?

Open the dashboard and select Start Chat. Live matching must be enabled for another user to connect.

How do I change my profile?

Open Account and select Edit Profile.

How do I log out?

Choose Sign Out in this menu or use the dashboard's logout button.

Why can't I see another person?

The current dashboard supports a local camera preview. Real-time connections require a video-matching system.

Terms & Privacy

Yappo should be used responsibly and respectfully.
